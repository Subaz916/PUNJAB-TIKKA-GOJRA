About us

Authentic Taste. Real BBQ. Punjab’s Flavor.

Punjab Jab Tikkah Gojra is focused on serving fresh, flavorful BBQ and traditional desi food with quality ingredients and a taste customers can enjoy with family and friends.

From smoky chicken tikkah and sizzling seekh kebabs to slow-cooked karahi and fragrant biryani — everything is prepared fresh, to order, with the flavours Punjab is known for.

  • Traditional desi recipes
